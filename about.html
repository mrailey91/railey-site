About Us

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc.

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc. is a leading provider of psychodiagnostic services and consultation in the Tallahassee, Florida area. Our team of experienced and dedicated professionals is committed to providing the highest quality diagnostic and evaluation services to individuals, families, and organizations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide comprehensive, evidence-based psychological evaluations that facilitate informed decision-making and improve outcomes for our clients. We are dedicated to delivering accurate diagnostic information, individualized treatment recommendations, and compassionate support throughout the evaluation process.

Our Team

Our team is comprised of highly skilled psychologists and professionals with extensive experience across a wide range of evaluation areas. Each member of our team brings specialized expertise and a commitment to providing thorough, objective, and compassionate assessments.

Our Approach

At Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc., we believe in a comprehensive, multi-method approach to evaluation. We use standardized assessments, clinical interviews, behavioral observations, and a thorough review of relevant records to ensure that our evaluations are accurate, thorough, and useful.

We understand that every individual is unique, and we tailor our evaluation process to meet the specific needs of each client. Our goal is not just to provide a diagnosis, but to offer meaningful insights and actionable recommendations that support each individual's growth and well-being.

Serving the Tallahassee Community

Located at 2236 Capital Circle Northeast, Suite 203, Tallahassee, FL 32308, we serve clients throughout the Tallahassee area and surrounding communities. We are open Monday through Friday from 9 am to 5 pm, with Saturday appointments available.
