Adult Forensic Services

Our forensic services for adults focus on providing comprehensive evaluations and assessments. Our evaluations are conducted in accordance with the relevant legal standards, and we provide objective and detailed reports to assist the court in making informed decisions.

Our forensic team is comprised of experienced and highly trained professionals who are committed to providing accurate and unbiased evaluations to help the court make fair and just decisions.

Our Forensic Services Include

  • Competency to stand trial evaluations
  • Criminal responsibility (insanity) evaluations
  • Risk assessment and violence risk evaluation
  • Mitigation evaluations for sentencing
  • Personal injury psychological evaluations
  • Disability and workers' compensation evaluations
  • Expert witness testimony
  • Consultation to attorneys and legal professionals

Our Approach

We utilize evidence-based assessment instruments and follow established forensic psychology guidelines. Our reports are thorough, objective, and clearly address the specific legal questions at issue. We are experienced in providing expert testimony and working within the adversarial legal framework.
