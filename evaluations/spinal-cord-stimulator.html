Spinal Cord Stimulator Evaluations

Competent Evaluations for Spinal Cord Stimulator Implants

At Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc., individuals seeking spinal cord stimulator (SCS) treatment can receive comprehensive psychological evaluations. The evaluations assess an individual's mental health and determine their readiness for the procedure.

The evaluations also help identify any potential barriers to success following the procedure. By partnering with Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc., individuals can receive the support they need to achieve long-term success and an improved quality of life.

What We Evaluate

  • Current psychological functioning and mental health status
  • History of chronic pain and its psychological impact
  • Coping strategies and pain management techniques
  • Expectations for the SCS procedure and outcomes
  • Substance use history and risk factors
  • Support systems and rehabilitation readiness

Why This Evaluation Matters

Psychological evaluations for spinal cord stimulator implantation are typically required by insurance companies and referring physicians. Research shows that psychological factors significantly influence outcomes for SCS procedures. Our evaluation helps ensure that candidates are well-prepared and have the psychological resources needed for a successful outcome.
