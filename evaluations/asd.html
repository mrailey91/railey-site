Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

Providing Assistance with Autism Spectrum Disorder

Our team specializes in comprehensive autism spectrum disorder (ASD) evaluations for both children and adults. We use a combination of standardized assessments, clinical interviews, and behavioral observations to accurately diagnose ASD and develop individualized treatment recommendations.

We understand that ASD affects each person uniquely and we are committed to providing a thorough assessment that considers the whole person. Our evaluations are designed to identify strengths and challenges and to inform treatment planning that supports each individual's goals and potential.

Our Approach

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc. uses evidence-based assessment tools and follows best-practice guidelines for ASD evaluation. Our process includes gathering developmental history, conducting standardized diagnostic instruments, observing behavior in structured and unstructured settings, and integrating information from multiple sources including parents, teachers, and other providers.

Who We Evaluate

We provide ASD evaluations for individuals of all ages, from toddlers to adults. Whether you are seeking an initial diagnosis, a second opinion, or an updated evaluation, our team is equipped to provide a thorough and compassionate assessment.

What to Expect

  • Comprehensive clinical interview and developmental history
  • Standardized diagnostic assessments
  • Behavioral observations
  • Cognitive and adaptive functioning measures
  • Detailed written report with individualized recommendations
  • Feedback session to discuss results and next steps
