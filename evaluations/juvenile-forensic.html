Juvenile Forensic Evaluations

Juvenile Forensic Services

Our juvenile forensic services address the unique needs of youth involved in the legal system. We understand that adolescents are developmentally different from adults, and our evaluations reflect an understanding of adolescent brain development, maturity, and the potential for rehabilitation.

Our evaluations are conducted in accordance with the relevant legal standards, and we provide objective and detailed reports to assist the court in making informed decisions about juvenile cases.

Our Juvenile Forensic Services Include

  • Competency to proceed evaluations for juveniles
  • Transfer/waiver evaluations (juvenile to adult court)
  • Risk assessment for juvenile offenders
  • Disposition and treatment recommendations
  • Amenability to treatment evaluations
  • Psychological evaluations for juvenile court
  • Expert witness testimony in juvenile proceedings

Developmental Considerations

Working with juveniles requires special expertise in adolescent development, including understanding of brain maturation, peer influence, identity formation, and the capacity for change. Our evaluations take these developmental factors into account when assessing competency, responsibility, and risk, providing the court with nuanced, developmentally informed assessments.
