Learning Disability Evaluations

Customized Plans for Learning Disability Evaluations

We provide comprehensive assessment and treatment services for individuals with learning disabilities. Our expert team of professionals conducts thorough evaluations to identify learning difficulties and provides customized treatment plans that focus on building strengths and addressing weaknesses.

We work collaboratively with clients and families to provide support and guidance and utilize evidence-based interventions to help individuals achieve their goals. With our individualized approach and commitment to excellence, our team is dedicated to helping individuals with learning disabilities achieve their full potential.

What We Assess

  • Reading skills (decoding, fluency, comprehension)
  • Written expression (spelling, grammar, composition)
  • Mathematical skills (calculation, reasoning, problem-solving)
  • Cognitive abilities (intelligence, processing speed, working memory)
  • Academic achievement relative to cognitive potential
  • Attention and executive functioning as they relate to learning

Our Reports Include

We provide detailed reports with specific recommendations for accommodations, interventions, and educational planning. Our reports are suitable for school-based IEP and 504 plan meetings, college accommodations applications, and workplace accommodation requests.
