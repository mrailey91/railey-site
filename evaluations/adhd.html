Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

ADHD: Customized Treatment Plans and Ongoing Support

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc. offers evaluation and consultation services for Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), providing comprehensive assessments, creating individualized treatment plans, and offering ongoing support to individuals, families, and healthcare providers. Our approach is evidence-based and tailored to the unique needs of each client.

Our assessments are designed to determine whether ADHD is present and, if so, what type. We use a multi-method approach that includes standardized rating scales, cognitive and neuropsychological testing, clinical interviews, and behavioral observation.

Our Assessment Process

Understanding the nuances of ADHD is critical to developing an effective treatment plan. Our evaluations provide detailed information about an individual's cognitive profile, including attention, memory, processing speed, and executive functioning, to guide personalized recommendations.

  • Comprehensive clinical interview
  • Standardized ADHD rating scales from multiple informants
  • Cognitive and neuropsychological testing
  • Behavioral observation
  • Review of academic, medical, and psychological records
  • Detailed report with diagnosis and treatment recommendations

Children, Adolescents, and Adults

ADHD presents differently across the lifespan. In children, it may manifest as difficulty staying focused in school, excessive physical activity, or impulsive behavior. In adults, ADHD often looks like difficulty with organization, time management, and sustained attention. Our evaluations are tailored to the developmental stage and life context of each individual.
