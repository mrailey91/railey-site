Bariatric Surgery Evaluations

Expert Psychological Evaluations for Bariatric Surgery

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc. offers expert psychological evaluations for bariatric surgery. These evaluations help individuals and their surgeons make informed decisions by assessing their psychological readiness for the procedure and the impact it will have on their life.

Our evaluations assess emotional readiness, eating behaviors, mental health history, and coping skills. We provide recommendations to help candidates prepare for and maintain success after surgery.

What We Assess

  • Psychological readiness for surgery and lifestyle changes
  • Current and past eating behaviors and patterns
  • Mental health history and current functioning
  • Coping skills and support systems
  • Understanding of surgical risks and post-operative requirements
  • Motivation and expectations for weight loss surgery

Why Psychological Evaluations Are Required

Most surgeons and insurance companies require a psychological evaluation before approving bariatric surgery. This evaluation ensures that candidates understand the significant lifestyle changes required, have realistic expectations, and are psychologically prepared for the challenges of the post-surgical period.
