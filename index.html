Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

Our team specializes in comprehensive autism spectrum disorder (ASD) evaluations for both children and adults. We use a combination of standardized assessments, clinical interviews, and behavioral observations to accurately diagnose ASD and develop individualized treatment recommendations.

We understand that ASD affects each person uniquely and we are committed to providing a thorough assessment that considers the whole person. Our evaluations are designed to identify strengths and challenges and to inform treatment planning that supports each individual's goals and potential.

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Forensic Psychology Solutions, Inc. provides comprehensive ADHD evaluations for children, adolescents, and adults. Our assessments are designed to determine whether ADHD is present and, if so, what type. We use a multi-method approach that includes standardized rating scales, cognitive and neuropsychological testing, clinical interviews, and behavioral observation.

Understanding the nuances of ADHD is critical to developing an effective treatment plan. Our evaluations provide detailed information about an individual's cognitive profile, including attention, memory, processing speed, and executive functioning, to guide personalized recommendations.

Fitness for Duty

Our fitness for duty evaluations are designed to assess whether an employee is psychologically capable of performing the essential functions of their job. These evaluations are commonly requested by employers, HR departments, and legal teams when there are concerns about an employee's mental health or behavior in the workplace.

We conduct thorough assessments using standardized psychological testing, clinical interviews, and a review of relevant records. Our reports provide clear, objective findings and recommendations to help employers make informed decisions.

Bariatric Surgery Psychological Evaluations

We provide pre-surgical psychological evaluations for individuals considering bariatric (weight loss) surgery. These evaluations are typically required by surgeons and insurance companies to ensure candidates are psychologically prepared for the significant lifestyle changes associated with bariatric surgery.

Our evaluations assess emotional readiness, eating behaviors, mental health history, and coping skills. We provide recommendations to help candidates prepare for and maintain success after surgery.

Learning Disability Evaluations

Our learning disability evaluations are designed to identify specific learning disorders that may be affecting academic performance. We assess reading, writing, math, and other academic skills using standardized instruments, and integrate cognitive testing to understand the underlying processes that contribute to learning difficulties.

We provide detailed reports with specific recommendations for accommodations, interventions, and educational planning.

We provide comprehensive child custody evaluations that assess the best interests of the child. Our evaluations consider parenting skills, parent-child relationships, and the developmental needs of the child.

Competency & Decision-Making Capacity

Our decision-making capacity evaluations assess an individual's ability to understand, appreciate, and reason about decisions related to medical treatment, financial management, or legal matters. These evaluations are often requested when there are concerns about cognitive decline or mental illness affecting an individual's ability to make informed decisions.

We provide clear, thorough reports that address the specific functional abilities at issue and offer recommendations for support and intervention.
